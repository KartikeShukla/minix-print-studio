# Code of Conduct

## Our Standards

Participants are expected to keep discussions respectful, technical, and constructive.
Good-faith disagreement is welcome; harassment, personal attacks, and repeated
disruption are not.

Examples of expected behavior:

- Focus criticism on ideas, code, tests, and evidence.
- Be explicit about safety risks and uncertainty.
- Respect privacy when sharing diagnostics or hardware artifacts.
- Help keep the project usable for new contributors.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, threats, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing another person's private information.
- Pressuring maintainers or users to run unsafe hardware tests.
- Sharing unredacted tokens, local paths, or diagnostics that contain private data.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, reject contributions, or restrict
participation when behavior harms project safety or contributor trust.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns through the project's private security or maintainer contact
channel. Do not include private hardware artifacts or tokens in public reports.
